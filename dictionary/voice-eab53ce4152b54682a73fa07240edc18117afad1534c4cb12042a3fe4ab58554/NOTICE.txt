RiNO matched the recordings to the displayed spelling and kana, retained their
spoken timing, trimmed surrounding silence, adjusted levels, and encoded the
clips as 96 kbps mono MP3. In this directory, recordings.json lists the source
and original audio file for each matched reading|spelling. For bare kanji with
okurigana, the recording is the complete indicated reading (e.g. 浴・あ.びる).
